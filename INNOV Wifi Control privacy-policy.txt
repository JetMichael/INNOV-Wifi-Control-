2. Data Collection and Use

iLink (hereinafter "the App") is the IoT device management and monitoring application for the INNOV brand. It connects INNOV devices through the cloud, displays battery / device runtime data, and sends anomaly alarms. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2.1 Information We Collect

Account Information:

Username and password provided at registration or login (used for account authentication)
Role information (Operator / Admin / Super Admin)
Device and Runtime Data:

Device identifier (deviceId), device name, online status, last active time
Real-time device telemetry: State of Charge (SOC), total voltage, total current, max / min temperature, State of Health (SOH), max / min cell voltage
Historical device records (used for trend charts)
Provisioning Information:

Device ID and device password read during Bluetooth provisioning
WiFi SSID and password you manually enter during BluFi provisioning
App Usage Data:

App settings and preferences (e.g., language selection, alarm threshold configurations)
App crash logs and performance data (for improving app stability)
Device model and operating system version (for compatibility optimization)
2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

Personal data such as contacts, photos, SMS, or call logs
Precise location (except the coarse location permission required by Android for Bluetooth scanning; we do not actively collect your location)
Payment information or financial data
3. Purpose of Data Use

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

Core Functionality: Connecting to and managing INNOV devices, and viewing real-time and historical device data through the cloud
Device Provisioning: Connecting devices to your WiFi network via Bluetooth / BluFi
Anomaly Alarms: Pushing local notifications when SOC, voltage, temperature, or SOH exceed configured thresholds
Multi-user Collaboration: Managing sub-accounts and permissions within the account system
App Improvement: Analyzing crash and performance data to enhance app stability
4. Data Storage and Protection

4.1 Data Storage

Device telemetry and account data are transmitted through APIs and stored on INNOV's cloud servers for cross-device synchronization and historical queries
To improve the experience on weak networks, some device lists and history are cached locally on your device (within the app sandbox)
Device provisioning credentials are used only during provisioning and are not uploaded to the cloud
4.2 Data Security

Account credentials are transmitted through APIs at login and stored / verified by the server
Communications use system network and Bluetooth security mechanisms
Local caches are stored in the app sandbox and cleared when the app is uninstalled
5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We commit to:

Not selling, trading, or transferring your personal data to any third parties
Not sharing data with advertising platforms or data analytics companies
Disclosing information only when required by law
6. Permission Usage Explanation

Bluetooth Permission: Used to scan nearby devices, complete WiFi provisioning (BluFi), and read CAN bus data over Bluetooth. This is the core functionality of the app.

Location Permission: Android requires location permission for Bluetooth scanning, used to discover nearby provisionable devices; we do not use it for positioning.

Camera Permission: Used to scan QR codes for quick device binding (QR codes are parsed locally and not uploaded).

Notification Permission: Used to push device anomaly alarms (e.g., overvoltage, over-temperature, low battery) to help you stay informed of device status.

Storage Permission: Used to save CAN bus recordings as files and to manage the local cache.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

Right to Access: View bound devices and runtime data within the app
Right to Deletion: Clear the local cache by uninstalling the app; server-stored device data can be deleted upon request to us
Right to Control: Revoke Bluetooth, location, camera, notification, or storage permissions at any time in your device's system settings
8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
